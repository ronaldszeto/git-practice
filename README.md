## Can AI Really Help Predict Recidivism and Help with Rehabilitation Efforts?
(https://www.crimlawpractitioner.org/post/can-ai-really-help-predict-recidivism-and-help-with-rehabilitation-efforts)

## What I found interesting

A few years back during high school, I wrote a research paper on the use of AI in predicting recidvism. When I first researched on the topic there was a lot of doubt of its viability and if it would be fair. My research found that although it showed promise, there were simply too many problems such as bias based on race and other factors. 

Now, a few years later, this article still describes the risks and bias present. The use of AI helps provide rehabilitation efforts to more individuals that are in need of it as it would typically take a long time for humans to check them. However, the bias in harshly punishing certain groups of people due to the training data being biased still show that it must require human supervision.
